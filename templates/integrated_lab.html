1. Course Summary

This course introduces the fundamentals of Computer Vision, covering image formation, preprocessing, feature extraction, and segmentation techniques. It also explores machine learning models for image analysis and introduces the concept of Digital Twins in real-world vision-based applications.

3. Course Outcomes (COs)

After the successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

  • Recall and explain the evolution, architecture, challenges, industry trends, and Human Visual System of computer vision.
  • Apply digital image acquisition, colour modelling, spatial filtering, geometric transformations, and frequency-domain analysis techniques to enhance and restore images.
  • Analyze image textures and salient features by implementing and comparing classical descriptors with deep CNN feature extractors (AlexNet, VGG, ResNet).
  • Evaluate segmentation strategies and Vision Transformers (ViTs) by assessing their trade-offs in complex real-world scenarios.
  • Design vision-enabled solutions integrating Digital Twins, Edge AI, and multimodal data for diagnostics, robotics, and smart-environment deployments.

4. Course Contents

Unit 1: Introduction: History of Computer Vision Classical to Deep Learning, Applications of Computer Vision, Challenges and Recent Advances, Industry trends. Introduction to image processing, Human Visual system.
Unit 2: Digital Image Fundamentals: Human Visual System, A simple image model, Image Acquisition, Sampling and quantization, Relationships between pixels, Colour models: RGB, HSI, YUV Colour models.
Unit 3: Image Enhancement & Restoration: Geometric Transformations Affine & Projective; Fundamentals spatial processing: Point, Local and Global Operations, Spatial filters, Frequency filtering: Smoothing, Sharpening. Image Restoration, Denoising and Reconstruction Overview. Image features: Edge Detection, line, contours, and corner detection. (Marr-Hildreth, LoG, DoG, Canny, Harris).
Unit 4: Texture Analysis: Overview, Textures Features & Descriptors, Texture Representation, Grey level Co-occurrence matrix, Texture Analysis. Salient feature Detection and Matching: Overview, SIFT, SURF, ORB, CNN feature extraction, Transfer learning with Pre-trained networks.
Unit 5: Image Segmentation: Region Growing, Region split, Region Merging, Segmentation as clustering: K-Means, Gaussian Mixture Model. Semantic Segmentation, Instance Segmentation, Panoptic Segmentation. Introduction to Transformers, Vision Transformers: ViTs, DINO, Patch-based Tokenization, Self-Attention for Images.
Unit 6: Digital Twining: Introduction to Digital Twin, DT Architecture and Frameworks, Modeling and Simulation. Edge AI: Real-time vision on embedded systems and mobile devices, Multimodal Learning: Integrating vision with text, audio, and sensor data, IoT Integration: Vision-powered smart environments and industrial automation. Case studies: Automated diagnosis, Inspection (Factory monitoring: Analyse components for deviations, Robot vision, and Obstacle avoidance.
